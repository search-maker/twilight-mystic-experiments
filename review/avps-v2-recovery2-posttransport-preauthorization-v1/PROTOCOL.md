# AVPS v2 recovery2 — post-transport dynamic preauthorization

## Purpose

This is a fresh solver-free preauthorization gate required after merge of the preregistered native authorization-seed transport implementation. It repeats the recovery2 candidate-seed and global scientific-ordinal surfaces against the new public `main` before any new authorization identity may be allocated.

The frozen scientific design is unchanged: 360 cases, 72 CRN groups, five independently defined OPAC profile states, 20,000,000 photon histories per case, and the existing geometry, wavelength, optical-property, estimator, aggregation, classification and result-opening rules. Ordinals 41 and 42 and all of their seeds, authorization/dispatch identities and failed attempt-1 runs remain permanently consumed and non-reusable.

The recovery2 candidate identity remains canonical seed SHA-256 `38c074fe01bd6d09fa7dc78af1ad323e2f42b606ca992c2950b8fc1f5b343a9f` and row-canonical SHA-256 `a88b28dcfaaeb354f294d1705a0f8ddbcd061083f277a038ab8c9dace44d9954`. Candidate values are review-workspace/artifact data only and are not tracked as a scientific authorization.

## Required review

The pull-request review must bind exact post-transport main, exact branch/head and exact three-file review scope; prove merged PR #643 and the native transport helper byte; bind merged PR #639 and its successful seed-freshness proof; validate the consumed ordinal-42 seed ledger only at its native historical authorization-head path; rebuild the same 72 recovery2 candidate seeds; repeat tracked-tree and snapshot-fenced two-pass repository-global seed scans; dynamically derive the next unused global scientific ordinal from the authoritative observation surface without hard-coding 43; reject any already-existing proposed authorization/dispatch ref or ordinal marker; and perform a final unchanged-head/ordinal recheck before PASS.

## Boundary

PASS means only that a separately reviewed fresh authorization-control/allocation gate may consume this proof if a fresh authorization-time recheck still passes. This package does not allocate an ordinal, create authorization or dispatch refs, apply candidate seeds to cases, execute libRadtran/MYSTIC/uvspec, open AVPS results, admit vertical profile/spectral AOD/SSA/phase into Level-B, access protected holdouts, use Taylor/Jerusalem residuals, or change production behavior.
