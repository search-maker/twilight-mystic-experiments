Primary phenomenon is a *human unaided-eye first-direct-detection interval* for a catalogued star, bracketed by the **last directly tested NOT_SEEN** followed by a **first DIRECT_SEEN**, repeated DIRECT_SEEN confirmation. - Site/horizon, stars, dates and instrument lists are unresolved and SHALL be completed in a **separate pre-outcome enrollment lock receipt**. Public repos receive pseudonymous site/observer IDs and cryptographic hashes, not private participant names, precise coordinates or consent attachments.
- Select exactly three distinct HIP targets per site, without looking at outcomes, from an immutable Johnson V catalog and precomputed ephemeris. Fixed V strata: `(-1,1]`, `(2,3]`, `(4,5.5]`, one per stratum if geometry permits. Need pre-registered site/date night visibility, target altitude **20–80 deg**, azimuth, unobstructed local horizon and source-specific geometry before actual sampling; if no valid candidate in a stratum, predeclare no target, **do not cherry-pick replacements after data**. Target selection algorithm: ascending HIP numeric ID among candidates sorted by minimum predicted angular separation from Moon (larger separation first), then HIP ascending; compute entirely from fixed input snapshots, independent of the model's visibility prediction. Freeze exact resulting HIP/site/date selection in receipt before data. No planets, no <20° targets in this P1.
- On each eligible target session begin sufficiently early to obtain **at least two independently time-stamped true NOT_SEEN looks**; observer checks same star every **20–30 sec** as practicable with no bright screen exposure while searching. Direct YES requires a second direct YES within **120 sec**. Preserve POSSIBLE, AVERTED_SEEN, CLOUD_OBSCURED, NOT_CHECKED without relabeling them as a direct YES/NO. Maintain timestamp for **each look**, not a session time. Every decision is recorded with a voice/independent timekeeper or shielded logger and hash-stored raw original. No phone display of the model prediction to observers. A second observer records separately without coordination.
- Admission of an actual onset bracket requires >=2 prior NOT_SEEN checks, known source and clock, **last NOT_SEEN to first DIRECT_SEEN <=120 seconds**, absolute UTC clock bound **<=5 seconds** for *each* event, first DIRECT_SEEN confirmed as above; both endpoints within Sun depression **2–16 degrees** and target altitude 20–80°, unobstructed target direction. Ambiguous or missed check is retained with censoring reason but not treated as a bracket. All observations when Sun depth >16° are retained as out of v1 scope. These values are **P1-only**, not an implicit V0/R1 merger.
- For every check preserve original timezone-offset aware UTC, clock synchronization method and error bound, catalog ID/V-band magnitude, site latitude/longitude/elevation and directional horizon in **private custody**, star alt/az and Sun depression computed reproducibly from a locked ephemeris, directional cloud/haze and Moon, artificial lights, adaptation time and recent screen/light exposure, observer method, weather, instrument readings **with exact viewing direction/passband/FOV/calibration**, and independently sourced coincident aerosol/AOD/HSRL/EarthCARE quality or explicit UNKNOWN. Unknown inputs stay UNKNOWN and may make numerical scoring ineligible; cannot impute atmosphere from a skyglow-only zenith reading.
