## B. Bounded observer session (only after gates)

Design maximum: 3 consented observers x 3 authorized **weekday** nights x 2 predefined stars = **18 observer-star events**; up to five site attempts or eight weeks, whichever occurs first; no replacement sampling for unexpected outcomes, cancellations, clouds or failures. Each event has independently selected HIP target and star magnitude, sky direction/alt-az and season. Select 20–80 degree altitude and V roughly +1 to +5.5 before outcomes, using a deterministic ephemeris-only selection rule (NOT the model prediction). No guarantee that this samples solar depths 10.5–16 degrees.

- One observer points at one named target from a non-predictive printed star chart while a separate timekeeper/audio recorder records UTC. Naked eye only; note direct versus averted vision, corrective glasses and every binocular/telescope use (which disqualifies unaided-eye timing). No displayed app, predictor values, or countdown.
- Begin at geometric solar-center depression 3 degrees; attempt target identification at intervals **no longer than 120 seconds** until d=16 degrees or a documented end. Say audibly exact target token and **NOT_SEEN / POSSIBLE / CONFIRMED / OBSCURED / NOT_CHECKED**. Timestamp each check in UTC; do not retroactively round to preset time slots. Recheck a first `CONFIRMED` within 120 seconds, while retaining the exact first time as R. Record two preceding NOT_SEEN attempts as needed for usable onset brackets.
- Calibrate UTC clock before and after each session; require <=10 s documented bound. Preserve original audio (only if separately consented), ink sheets or immutable device file privately and SHA-256 hash the raw file prior to transcription.
- At least every five minutes and immediately on a transition: UTC, target HIP, alt/az, clouds and cirrus *at target*, transparency, horizon and moon state, local lights/screens, adaptation-start and last bright-light time, temperature/humidity, and whether altitude is blocked. Optional directional calibrated SQM measurement: store actual pointing, FoV/bandpass and UTC; zenith measurements labeled `ZENITH_ONLY` with **no** directional or human-visibility inference. Aerosol source needs actual sample time, QC status, distance/representativeness and measured source bytes; if unknown, write `AEROSOL_UNKNOWN`, never estimate an AOD to fill a blank.
- Stop on safety concern, site restriction, cloud obstruction, light/adaptation reset, equipment failure, Shabbat/Yom Tov blackout or the finite cap; retain and label aborted sessions and every not-seen event.

## E. Immutable decision rules (these are proposed now, before any outcome)

Admit an onset bracket **[L,R]** only when L is the last target-specific NOT_SEEN before first corroborated unaided-eye CONFIRMED at R, at least two prior NOT_SEEN checks exist, R-L <=150 seconds, a second CONFIRMED occurs within 120 seconds, UTC bound <=10 s and no obscuration/bright-light breach in the bracket. First checked and already seen = LEFT_CENSORED; never seen by exit = RIGHT_CENSORED; cloud, aid, identity uncertainty, broken adaptation or missing original = NOT_ADMITTED. Both included as denominators, not dropped. Count one observer-star-night event once, not every repeated check.

For a prelocked supported prediction instant P, interval-distance error in minutes is `max((L-P)/60,0,(P-R)/60)`; also publish signed outside-bracket residual, full bracket widths, median and 80th percentile of distance, null/unsupported proportions, and observer/night clusters. No prediction when frozen model says null: report UNSUPPORTED, **not** an error of zero. Pilot feasibility requires >=12/18 admitted from >=2 observers and >=2 nights, >=90% original retention; preliminary prediction screen (not release qualification) median interval-distance <=5 minutes and >=80% of supported predictions within 10 minutes of their brackets. At least six valid brackets entirely within d in (10.5,16] required even to describe that regime as **pilot sampled**. Pilot success is **not** evidence of universally minute-accurate release.
