## Finite candidate prospective design — freeze BEFORE recruitment/outcomes

- Study unit: **observer × date × identified star**, 3 consented observers × 3 permitted weekday nights × 2 stars = **maximum 18 target-events**. Maximum **5 site visits or 8 weeks**, whichever first, including aborted weather visits; zero paid equipment, commitments, unsolicited site access or MYSTIC runs. Cancellations and no-visibility events count toward the fixed sample/stop rule. Where fewer than 3 observers enroll, report actual N and inability to meet gate, **do not refill opportunistically**.
- Site and privacy: physical site must be approved by its custodian and recorded in a privately access-controlled original record; GNSS coords/height and horizon photograph/profile fixed before observing, **never posted with personally identifying details publicly**. Multiple sites require separate site identifiers; do not silently transfer calibration.
- Target selection *before outcomes*: a catalogue version and exact target IDs/coordinates/magnitudes are fixed by an ephemeris-only selector, **not selected by model predicted onset or outcome**. Aim to include stars of roughly magnitude +1 through +5.5 and altitudes 20°–80° at evening solar depths 4°–16°; exclude targets below 20°, above 80° or whose identity cannot reliably be differentiated visually. This does not guarantee sightings at 10.5°–16°; record the actual post-hoc solar-depth stratum mechanically without changing admission rules.
- Sampling per observer: each observer gets **two named targets** on a static paper chart, plus an independent audio/timekeeper, but **never sees model predicted times, depths, threshold curves or a countdown**. Attempt each target at least every **120 s**, from solar depth 3° until depth 16°, target obscured, or safety cessation. Record **NOT_SEEN**, **SUSPECTED**, **SEEN**, and any **SKIPPED**, each with raw UTC timestamp and reason. Require two distinct SEEN reports within 120 s of each other for onset corroboration; keep the first SEEN as interval right endpoint. Never transform 'first checked and already seen' into first visibility.
- First-sighting interval: **[last NOT_SEEN before first corroborated SEEN, first SEEN]**; require at least two prior NOT_SEEN checks, <=150 s bracket width, clock verified <=10 s, and target-specific sky conditions without an intervening light/adaptation breach. If any fails, retain as **left-censored / right-censored / condition-compromised / not-admitted**, never delete or treat as a point-time. Potential first sightings are a human protocol outcome, not a guaranteed objective retinal threshold.
- Environment: every 5 min and immediately at any visibility transition log target identity, pointing alt/az, cloud near each target (oktas and cirrus), transparency, local lamps/headlights/screens, moon/horizon, humidity/temperature and observer light-adaptation history. If available, collect **directional** calibrated SQM measurement at the same target sky patch (pointing + actual measurement UTC + field of view/bandpass), and **separately** label zenith SQM. A 90° zenith SG_088/whole-sky camera measurement by itself **cannot** validate a <=80° target-direction radiance or human first sighting. No SQM value may be fabricated to complete a field.
- Aerosols: catalogue original station/profile, UTC sample times, QC level, height geometry, distance to site, and measured AOD spectral conversion/method. NASA AERONET Halifax is currently a *site locator* only; it is **not automatically colocated with SCO**, and daylight AOD cannot simply be asserted at night. For direct atmosphere-admitted comparison require documented local or representative sampled aerosol profile; if absent, flag **AEROSOL_UNKNOWN** and retain a descriptive field event, *not a full physical-input-validated case*.
- Existing source-compatible schema: [V0 target-condition amendment](https://github.com/search-maker/twilight-mystic-experiments/blob/science/v0-target-condition-admission-20261119/review/first-visibility-pilot-20261109/field_records.V0_TARGET_CONDITION_PRE_ENROLLMENT_PROPOSAL.json) has ENROLLMENT / SESSION / CONDITION / CHECK / PREDICTION_LOCK records. Keep original audio, raw CSV and source bytes in restricted custody and hash originals. Existing draft is **not** itself an approved enrollment contract.
