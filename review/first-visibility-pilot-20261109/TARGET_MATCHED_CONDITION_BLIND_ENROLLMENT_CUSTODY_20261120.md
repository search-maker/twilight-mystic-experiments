## Newly verified original design flaw and evidence

The actual V0 `field_records.schema.json` (Git blob `d1be04c0dd43c0653b98f4e4d0fada580e8383a1`, pinned commit `ba214a351c8f633e87892e1360bcaadff860d4ce`) defines `CHECK` with `session_token, check_token, target_id, timestamp_utc`, but `CONDITION` has **only session and timestamp**, although `cloud_near_target_oktas` is named as if it identifies a star. V0 permits **up to three different HIP targets at the same site**. Thus a cloud or sky-condition value in a mixed-star session is not attributable to the **specific first-NO/first-YES target** from these records alone. In particular, a 90-degree SG_088/zenith measurement cannot replace target-direction brightness at <=80 degrees, and neither can establish naked-eye first appearance.

Source custody: V0 prepared prereg blob `ef160b7357886df1c59b3cbd9f8d857ef6617cbf`; R1 field card `4e18fd78251fae4efa6f2313966196375aed2e78`; prior V0/R1 conflicting criteria audit `64fab5088363376284f9f6c289565c122a93943e`; existing **blank** provenance/enrollment manifest `bea5da86a72768b9421e8c2c4b862d914c030190`. None is registered, executed, or original human ground truth.

The proposed additional `matched_directional_condition_sidecar_20261120.schema.json` has exactly **40 required source-custody fields** and a one-to-one source join key `(session_token,check_token,target_catalog,target_id,timestamp_utc)`. It records actual star alt/az, target-cloud and obstruction, local lights, visual adaptation, interobserver cue contamination, raw original hashes, true AOD source/time/QC/distance, and optical pointing/FoV/calibration/units. Unknown atmosphere is literally `UNKNOWN` and nullable, **not AOD=0**. A `ZENITH_ONLY` measurement is forced to altitude 90 and cannot be represented as directional <=80. A valid schema does not prove an observation happened or the source is authentic. Actual field joins, original bytes, causality and QC still require independent review.
