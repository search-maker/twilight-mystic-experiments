## Before saying yes
- Participation is voluntary, unpaid, for adults at a safe, lawful **fixed site** that remains accessible from local sunset to Sun depression 16 degrees. No roofs or restricted public preserves. A second adult should be available to record observations **independently** without comparing answers. The pilot target is **2 distinct sites x 2 observers**, not a claim of enrollment.
- Research consent is separate from optional permission to publish **redacted** results and separate again from permission to retain/share audio. Declining public release or audio does not by itself exclude private, text-only scientific participation. Consent and site details must be recorded privately before a session; never send residential addresses, personal coordinates or raw voice recordings to public GitHub.
- Tell the existing Outreach Replies owner only whether you are interested, a site is safely available for the full evening twilight interval, and whether two independent adult observers could attend. **Do not collect study measurements yet.** The Coordinator must first freeze the exact runnable model commit and configuration, catalogue, accepted sites/dates/target list, uncertainty/acceptance rules, and encrypted hidden predictions *before* the first field check. You must not receive or look up predicted first-sighting times.

## On an approved night (after a separate go-ahead)
1. Take the fixed target-name/pointing list from the Coordinator; up to 3 preselected stars (HIP IDs, with catalogue magnitude locked privately). A direction/altitude chart may guide finding the field, **not** tell you when the star is expected. Do not use the public calculator, model time, a visibility alert, binoculars, telescope or enhanced camera to decide visibility. Normal prescription glasses are allowed and recorded. Never point instruments at the Sun.
2. Start around the **astronomically calculated local sunset**, not at a model-predicted first-seen time. Record a phone/GNSS/NTP clock check with estimated UTC error; each adult logs separately. The same star should receive an actual check about every **15–30 seconds when practicable**. Rotate among targets only if genuine check timestamps remain accurate; mark *NOT_CHECKED* where a target was not inspected.
3. For **each actual look**, save the device-side UTC timestamp, HIP target, and one of: **NOT_SEEN** (you really examined its position), **POSSIBLE**, **CONFIRMED**, **CONTINUOUS**, **OBSCURED**, or **NOT_CHECKED**. Do not backfill or guess. Require a second independent-in-time confirmation within 120 seconds for the first CONFIRMED result. A clouded or missed check is never a NOT_SEEN. No detection from optical equipment counts.
4. Note **direct gaze / averted gaze / unknown** for each look. If using averted gaze, optionally estimate the offset; do not call a gaze change a physical model success. Record when dark adaptation began and every bright-screen/light exposure. Never imply automatic 20-minute adaptation from time of sunset.
5. At the start and about every **5 minutes**, plus whenever conditions change, record clouds **near the particular star**, haze/smoke, Moon, artificial-light changes, horizon obstruction and observer height/site information. Air temperature/humidity and measured aerosols/AOD550 may be linked only with provenance and real times; otherwise record **UNKNOWN**, not zero or a default disguised as measurement.
6. Optional SQM-L: log actual reading time, calibration/device, field of view and pointing altitude/azimuth; a star-direction reading is distinct from zenith. **Neither SQM nor a whole-sky photometer can establish that a human saw a star.** Stop at Sun depression 16 degrees, unsafe conditions or weather, and retain unsuccessful/cloudy/censored sessions.

## Why negative checks matter
The primary outcome is an honest interval between the **last actual NOT_SEEN** and **first repeated CONFIRMED** observation of the same star by the same observer. Without both, no exact first-sighting time is known. A bracket over 120 seconds or contaminated by obscuration is not promoted to precise evidence. All unsuccessful attempts stay in the denominator.
