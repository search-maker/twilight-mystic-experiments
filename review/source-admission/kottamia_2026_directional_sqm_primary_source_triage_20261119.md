### What was measured, and what was NOT

- Site Kottamia Astronomical Observatory (KAO), **29°55′35.24″ N / 31°49′45.85″ E, altitude ~478 m**, Egypt.
- **Continuous zenith** SQM-LU-DL-V photometry **every 4 minutes**, Nov 2021–Jan 2023 (15 months). The study reports **~320–700 nm** instrument response and approximate **20° field of view**. Monthly results use moon-below-horizon selection; they are not individual-human observations.
- **Different four-night directional campaign**: 1–2, 2–3, 3–4 and 6–7 Aug 2022, three SQM units at **1-minute intervals**, zenith altitude **90°**, true-east azimuth 90° at **altitude 0°**, true-west azimuth 270° at **altitude 0°**. Dates/field setup are source-reported; original per-minute instrument CSVs, clock/serial/calibration audit and near-field horizon mask have NOT been obtained.
- Published Table 4 astronomical-evening phase (~**18:11–18:16 UT**) reports ranges in **mag_SQM/arcsec²**: **east 18.80–19.70**, **west 17.50–18.00**, **zenith 19.25–19.93**, Moon altitude **11.2–33.9°**. These are published phase-level ranges **NOT synchronized per-direction individual records**; do not pair extrema or turn differences into an exact physical flux ratio. Moon above horizon in the directional campaign, **unlike** the separately filtered long-term zenith averages.
- Atmospheric provenance: **MODIS/Terra MOD08_M3 v6.1** combined DT/DB **AOD550 1°×1° MONTHLY** grid, AIRS ozone and MODIS PWV monthly. Example **Aug 2022 monthly AOD550 = 0.28**, *not* 1–7 Aug event-specific AOD, a vertical profile, or an in-situ value. Paper reports monthly AOD vs zenith SQM correlation **r = -0.52 to -0.77** for solar elevation -6° to -15° (p<0.05), not a direct event-wise aerosol causality proof.
- **NO human observer first-star detection time**, no named target/magnitude, no NOT_SEEN→CONFIRMED bracket, no eyesight/visual-adaptation protocol. An SQM instrument cannot demonstrate naked-eye threshold. Zenith 90° cannot validate target alt ≤80°; east/west instruments point **0°**, not 5°–80° target directions. Their ~20° FOV may sample the near-horizon/below-horizon environment, so their angular response and obstruction/ground mask must be checked before a directional model comparison.
- This new work is directly relevant to *measured background sky behavior* near the missing **10.5°–16° Sun-depression range**, but does **NOT** validate human first visibility, exact min accuracy, spectral radiance in model units, or true matched atmospheric profiles.
