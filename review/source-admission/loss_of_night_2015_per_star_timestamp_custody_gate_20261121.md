## Why this is an independent, falsifiable acquisition target
The original organizer Christopher Kyba's 2015-08-07 invitation asked volunteers to make an **eight-star visual observation** during twilight (Aug 7–14) and to take measurements promptly *after the first star was out*. This is a real invitation for HUMAN naked-eye star classifications, not a zenith SG_088 meter. It does **NOT** establish that any individual star was first NOT_SEEN and then first SEEN, nor that individual check timestamps exist. Original: https://lossofthenight.blogspot.com/2015/08/first-community-experiment.html

NEW source verification (not repeated from prior source-admission note): the actual **2014-11-05 app v2.0 field manual** states (a) initial GPS position, one user-selected **weather** status before star search, (b) phone guides a particular star by compass/tilt, (c) an initial bright star is selected, (d) each star gets one human classification and a reason-menu for NOT_VISIBLE/UNSURE, (e) completion is after **8 stars**, and an observer can optionally add groups of 3, (f) the observation was sent to a **GLOBE at Night-hosted server**, and (g) during twilight the user saw a warning and must choose **'Measure anyway'** (described as a way to test). Original: https://lossofthenight.blogspot.com/2014/11/a-step-by-step-guide-to-using-loss-of.html . The **2014-11-18 original author note** distinguishes **clearly**, **barely**, **averted-vision only**, and **invisible** categories (plus unsure) and demonstrates 14-star decisions from a single observer on an earlier night. Original: https://lossofthenight.blogspot.com/2014/11/an-example-of-user-data-with-new.html . Do NOT treat that illustrative November 2014 set as first appearance or as August 2015 data.

The present https://www.myskyatnight.com/ FAQ says **twilight observations are excluded from the public map**, with GPS positions rounded to 0.001 degree for privacy; that map is not an archive of the target outcome universe. The current home page also reports it **cannot access new data** (live site condition only, not evidence old data are lost). The 2014 hosting statement is HISTORICAL, not a verified current custody assignment.
